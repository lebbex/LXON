Justification

Well structured data should always be readable without comments, same rule as with code. However, it's understandable the neee to comment on data when, for example, sharing it for debugging, writing config files, or simply reminding future you and others what something means or what it's supposed to look like.

In short, comments are something to avoid using, but are supported for the rare occurences you may want them.

Syntax

A comment is very straightforward to write. Simply begin a new line with a hashtag #, followed by a space, tab, or another hashtag. The rest of the line in full will be skipped by the parser.

You can also begin a line with a hashtag, followed by a newline, to skip an empty line. This is pretty much useless though, and is merely a quality of life feature.

Important

You must include the space character after the hashtag, or it will be interpreted as an !! val.col.space.srgb ! sRGB color value !, since they share declaration character.

Also, you cannot write inline comments, that means comments on the same line with data. Writing inline comments would go against the standard and will likely break on many parsers, including the officially released ones.
